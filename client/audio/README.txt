Hearthvale recorded audio

footsteps/
grass.wav — footsteps on grass
gravel.wav — footsteps on gravel paths
wood.wav — footsteps on floors and bridges

actions/
chop.wav — axe impact
mine.wav — pickaxe impact
splash.wav — fishing splash

combat/
hit.wav — combat impact

creatures/
bird.wav — short bird call
moo.wav — cow call

ambience/
wind.ogg — seamless wind loop
water.ogg — seamless water loop
fire.ogg — seamless campfire and furnace loop

Use WAV, OGG Vorbis or MP3 with these base filenames.
Short mono recordings work best for positional effects.
Ambience files should have seamless endpoints; the client loops them.
Restart the client after replacing files.
Missing files retain the temporary generated sounds.

The project audio folder is bundled into future builds.
An audio folder beside the EXE overrides bundled recordings without rebuilding.
Master, effects and ambience settings continue to apply.
